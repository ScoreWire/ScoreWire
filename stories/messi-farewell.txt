Lionel Messi will pull on Argentina's number 10 shirt for the last time on Tuesday, October 6, when the national team hosts Benin in a friendly at the Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires.

The 39-year-old announced his retirement from international football at the end of August, after Argentina lost the 2026 World Cup final 1-0 to Spain in extra time. Argentina's football federation, led by president Claudio Tapia, then invited him back for one more game so he could say goodbye in front of his home crowd.

## A farewell on home soil

Messi skipped the earlier friendlies against Bolivia and Burkina Faso and joins the squad only for this match, together with Rodrigo De Paul and Giovani Lo Celso. Players from the 2022 World Cup-winning squad and their families have also been invited, and the federation has planned a fan festival in Buenos Aires on the same day.

## The numbers behind the career

Messi enters the match with 207 caps and 125 goals for Argentina, both national records, and the game will be his 208th appearance. He made his debut in 2005, reached the World Cup final in 2014, and lifted the trophy in Qatar in 2022. At this year's tournament he scored eight goals and added four assists.

## What comes next

Stepping away from Argentina does not mean the end of his career. Messi remains at Inter Miami, where his contract runs through the 2028 MLS season. For coach Lionel Scaloni, the match also marks the start of a new chapter for the national team, with the departures of Messi and defender Nicolas Otamendi.
